Design kit · review 1

Who hosts the night?

Three host options, drawn in the kit's chunky flat kawaii style. Each one is shown asking a question and cheering, at full size and at 48px, on the paper card and on Midnight, the darkest surface. The giant Pacific octopus prize avatar sits beside each host so you can judge whether they look like one set.

The lead's pick: C, Rocco. He has the clearest silhouette at 48px (mask, ears and ringed tail), the most personality for a game that only happens after dark, and, as a park neighbor rather than an exhibit, he never competes with the prize animals. Pebble is a close second if you'd rather the host be an Academy animal.

Option A · an Academy animal

Pebble, the African penguin

Pebble is a grown-up African penguin from the Academy's colony, in a sky-blue bow tie, who works the room like a proud quizmaster. African penguins are the Academy's best-loved residents, so everyone in the bar knows them, and the pink brow and spotted chest make Pebble an African penguin rather than a generic cartoon one. The tuxedo already looks dressed for a night out. Penguins are noisy, social birds that live in a crowd, which suits a game played by teams. The tradeoff: the penguin chick is one of tonight's prizes, so host and prize share a species family.

Option B · a person

Dr. June, the night-shift scientist

Dr. June is the Academy biologist who stays late on Thursdays: big curly bun with a honey pencil tucked in, round honey glasses, lab coat over a sky-blue shirt. She's the friend who knows a fun fact about everything and can't wait to tell you. A scientist host says "this is a science museum" without a logo, lets every animal stay a prize instead of a rival, and gives the game a warm human face. She's the most expressive of the three, because a face with brows and glasses reads clearly at any size. The tradeoff: a person means skin tone and hair choices, and some players may read her as a real staff member.

Option C · a nocturnal neighbor

Rocco, the Golden Gate Park raccoon

Rocco lives in Golden Gate Park, right outside the Academy's doors, and Thursday night is his night. He's a nocturnal regular who sneaks into NightLife, a bit cheeky and very curious, with a striped tail he can't keep still. Raccoons are the night shift of the animal world, so he fits a game that only happens after dark, and he's a local San Francisco character, not an exhibit, so he never competes with the prize animals. His bandit mask and ringed tail make the strongest silhouette of the three at 48px. The tradeoff: he isn't an Academy animal, so the Academy link comes from the story, not the species.
