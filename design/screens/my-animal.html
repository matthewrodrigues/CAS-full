Professor Wiggles

Giant Pacific octopus · Enteroctopus dofleini

Named by The Blobfish Brigade on Nov 12

See it at Steinhart Aquarium, Northern California Coast

Can change the color and texture of its skin in a fraction of a second, and loves puzzle feeders.

Updates

  1. Nov 15

    Professor Wiggles spent the whole morning exploring a new rock pile the keepers built in the tank. It tucked itself into the deepest gap, turned the same rusty red as the rocks, and only came out when the crab treats arrived. Come by in the afternoon, when it's usually most active, and look low on the left side of the window.
